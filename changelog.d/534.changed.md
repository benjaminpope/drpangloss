`fit(method="adam")` now runs in jitted chunks of 200 steps (same trajectory, step for step), so `time_limit` and `progress` apply to it, and it reports convergence: `info["converged"]` is `True` if the `ftol_abs` plateau stop fired or `grad_norm <= gtol`, else `False` with `info["stop"]` `"limit"`, `"time"` or `"non-finite"` (it was `None`, so an Adam fit that hits `max_steps` now warns). New keywords: `adam_eps` (`optax.adam`'s `eps`), `ftol_abs` and `ftol_window` (stop when the loss fell by less than `ftol_abs` over the last `ftol_window` steps); `info["loss_history"]` is the unscaled loss before each Adam step. `ftol_window` below 1 raises `ValueError`.
